Gobbly.io – Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · Sultan of Niyyat

Gobbly.io is a casual game for Android by Sultan of Niyyat ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: we do not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Gobbly.io has no accounts and no sign-in, and it contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (your level, coins, boosters, collected cards and settings such as sound and vibration) on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. This information does not leave your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

You can buy coins, bundles and other items inside the game. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. We never see or store your payment details; the game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was made, so it can give you the item.

Internet and third parties

Gobbly.io works offline. It only connects to the internet through Google Play when you make or restore a purchase. The rankings, contests and races in the game are worked out on your device; nothing about you is sent to us or to other players.

Children

Gobbly.io is intended for players aged 13 and over. Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds a new feature, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before that version of the game is released.

Contact
