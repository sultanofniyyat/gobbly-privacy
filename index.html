Gobbly.io – Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · Sultan of Niyyat

Gobbly.io is a casual game for Android by Sultan of Niyyat ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: we never ask for your name, email or contacts, and we do not sell your information. The game uses one analytics service and one ad service, described below.

Information we collect

Gobbly.io has no accounts and no login, and it does not ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone. To improve the game and to show optional reward videos, two services receive some information from the game:

GameAnalytics (analytics): a random number for your device, which levels you start, win or lose, what you buy in the game and from which screen, your device model and system version, the game version, and error reports. We use it to see where the game is too hard or too easy and which offers work. It does not use the advertising identifier. GameAnalytics' policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Google AdMob (ads): the game offers short videos you can choose to watch for a reward, such as extra time or a free heart. There are no forced ads. To show these videos Google may receive your device's internet address (and from it your rough area), the advertising identifier, which ads were seen, how you used them, and error and performance data. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for your permission first, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → AD CHOICES. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (your level, coins, boosters, collected cards and settings such as sound and vibration) on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. Your progress itself stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

You can buy coins, bundles and other items inside the game. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. We never see or store your payment details; the game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was made, so it can give you the item.

Internet and third parties

Gobbly.io works offline. When it is online it talks to Google Play for purchases, to Google Play Games for sign-in and saving your progress in the cloud (Google's privacy policy applies), to GameAnalytics for the game statistics above, and to Google for the reward videos. The rankings, contests and races in the game are worked out on your device; nothing about you is sent to other players. We do not sell your information. To ask us to delete the game statistics linked to your device, write to the address below.

Children

Gobbly.io is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not in any children's category. We do not knowingly collect information from children; the reward videos are limited to ads suitable for general audiences.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds a new feature, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before that version of the game is released.
